# Stellar 14 glossary

Plain English for the words you heard today. This file is for the Content Crew, and for anyone who wants their first PR to be a docs PR.

There are two kinds of open work in here:

* A few words are spelled wrong on purpose. Fix **one** typo per PR (issue #18).
* Some terms say TODO. Pick **one** and write a definition a friend who has never coded would understand (issue #19).

Keep every definition to one or two short sentences.

## Account

A Stellar account is like a bank account on the network. It has an address that starts with G and holds your balances.

## Anchor

A business that connects Stellar to the normal money world, so you can turn local cash into a digital token and back again.
